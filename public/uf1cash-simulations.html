How to use these results

A high simulated chance of success is meaningful only if the probability and offered odds are real, independent and tested out of sample. Improve the model first; do not tune these sliders until the output looks profitable.

For correlated selections and baskets, the Python simulation engine requires a full joint outcome probability table. This browser tool intentionally models a single two-outcome ticket per round.
